---
name: Clue publication and history
description: Product behavior for clue review, immutable publication, retirement, and historical records.
---

Setters review the clue, answer, wordplay, attempt limit, and zero to three ordered hints before publishing. Once published, clue wording, answer, wordplay, attempt limit, and hints cannot be edited. Solvers reveal hints in order during an active run; each reveal adds 60 seconds to live and recorded solve time. Only already revealed hints may be returned to that solver.

Retiring a clue or blocking it after 100 distinct signed-in accounts report it hides it from public browsing and solving while preserving attempts, ratings, and leaderboard history. A person can report a clue once per account; setters cannot report their own clue.

Deleting an account removes its Clerk sign-in and public blurb but retains the reserved public ID, published clues, solves, and ratings under that ID. Do not remove the profile or ID reservation that anchors this history.

The user does not require multilingual moderation.

**Why:** The user requires immutable published clues, history-preserving retirement, account deletion that keeps clues and scores under a permanently reserved public ID, and an explicit one-minute cost for each hint reveal.

**How to apply:** Preserve immutable publication, unique-account reporting, and history-preserving retirement/blocking. Keep unused hint text private, persist each solver's revealed-hint count, and include its penalty in live and completed times. When deleting an account, remove its Clerk sign-in and blurb but retain the profile, ID reservation, and public history.
